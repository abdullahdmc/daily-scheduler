Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Daily scheduler is a free, open-source desktop app made by Md Abdullah Saeed Khan. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it's kept, and the choices you have. In short, your tasks stay on your computer. If you choose to sign in with Google, they're also copied to your own Google Tasks, and nowhere else.

Information stored on your computer

The app saves your tasks and settings, such as colour, window position and size, in a file on your computer:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Daily scheduler
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Daily scheduler
  • Linux: ~/.config/Daily scheduler

The developer has no servers and never receives this information. The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.

Google user data

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, the app asks Google for these permissions:

  • Your email address (openid, email): used only to show which Google account you're signed in with.
  • Google Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks): used to create a task list called "Daily scheduler" in your Google Tasks, and to add, update, complete and delete tasks in that list so it matches the app.

To find its own list, the app looks at the names of your task lists. It doesn't read or change the tasks in any other list. Tasks you add to the "Daily scheduler" list in Google Tasks, for example on your phone, are copied into the app.

How Google data is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is stored on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage where it's available. Copies of the synced tasks are kept in the same local file as your other tasks. Google data is sent only between your computer and Google's servers, over encrypted connections.

How Google data is shared

Google user data isn't shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone. It's not used for advertising, and it's not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. No person, including the developer, can read your tasks.

Daily scheduler's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Stop syncing: in the app, open Settings and choose Sign out. This removes the sign-in token from your computer and asks Google to revoke it.
  • Revoke access from Google: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Daily scheduler.
  • Delete synced tasks: delete the "Daily scheduler" list in Google Tasks.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app and delete the folder listed above.

Children

Daily scheduler isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Earlier versions are kept in the project's change history.
